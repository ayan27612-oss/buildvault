The workflow

Understand first. Change second.

BuildVault is designed around a simple principle: inspect the real project before touching it.

01 · Inspect Locate the relevant files and project structure.
02 · Understand Analyze dependencies, relationships, and existing behavior.
03 · Plan Determine the smallest safe change.
04 · Verify Check integration, references, and project integrity.
